### Section 9: Planned Data Synthesis

#### 9a. Formal Data Synthesis Planned?
- **Selection:** `🔘 Formal synthesis is planned`

#### 9b. Strategy for Data Synthesis (CTM Standard Biostatistical Text)
```text
Random-effects meta-analyses will be performed using the inverse-variance method for continuous variables and the Mantel-Haenszel method (or restricted maximum likelihood / Paule-Mandel estimation) for dichotomous variables. Continuous outcomes will be pooled as mean differences (MD) or standardized mean differences (SMD) if different scales are used. Dichotomous outcomes will be pooled as Risk Ratios (RR) or Odds Ratios (OR) with 95% confidence intervals.

Statistical heterogeneity will be assessed using the I² statistic, τ², and Cochran's Q test (with p < 0.10 indicating significant heterogeneity). Heterogeneity thresholds will be interpreted according to the Cochrane Handbook: 0-40% (low), 30-60% (moderate), 50-90% (substantial), and 75-100% (considerable). 

When studies report multiple correlated endpoints or multiple timepoints per subject, multilevel random-effects modeling (Robust Variance Estimation — RVE) will be applied to prevent artificial sample size inflation. Subgroup analyses and meta-regression will be performed if at least 10 studies (or >=3 studies per stratum) are available. Small-study effects and publication bias will be assessed via funnel plot visual asymmetry and Egger's test if at least 10 studies are included in a comparison. Statistical analyses will be executed in R (version 4.3+) using the 'meta' and 'metafor' packages.
```
