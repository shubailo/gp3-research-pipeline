# Introduction to Translational Medicine

## The Translational Medicine Model

The core objective of research in this context is to accelerate the translation of scientific results back to community benefits. This follows a cyclic four-element model:

1. **Healthcare (The Starting Point):** Clinical questions arise directly from healthcare practice and patient needs.
2. **Science:** Clinical questions are answered using basic, applied, or clinical scientific methodologies in an objective and reproducible way.
3. **Knowledge:** The acquired scientific results form new knowledge.
4. **Communication:** Knowledge must be communicated and disseminated to target audiences (practitioners, patients, policymakers) in an understandable format so it can be applied back to **Healthcare**.

> **WARNING**: If any element of this cycle is missing, no true healthcare improvement can be accomplished.

## Role of Systematic Reviews & Meta-Analyses (SRMAs)
SRMAs are a key part of the Translational Medicine model because they:
- Synthesize existing primary research objectively.
- Bridge the gap between **Science** and **Knowledge**.
- Serve as the foundation for **Communication** (e.g., guidelines) to improve **Healthcare**.

## Agent Objectives
When brainstorming or planning meta-analyses:
- Ensure the question originates from a real clinical/healthcare need.
- Ensure the synthesized knowledge will be clearly communicable and applicable in practice.
