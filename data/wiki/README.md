## 🏛️ Wiki Architecture and Purpose

This knowledge base is built upon two interconnected pillars:
1. **[Methodology & CTM Curriculum Repository (`methodology/`)](file:///c:/Users/shuba/Desktop/Researcher%20-%20D%C3%B3ri/data/wiki/methodology/INDEX.md):** The Semmelweis University Centre for Translational Medicine (CTM) 22-step master workflow, Cochrane Handbook v6.5 directives, data extraction standards, and biostatistical protocols (12 canonical chapters).
2. **Research Projects & Study Dossiers:** Transparent, study-level evidence sheets, raw verification dossiers, and BoostR extraction workbooks for ongoing systematic reviews.

## 🔬 2. Research Projects & Study Dossiers (Local Working Copies - Gitignored)

Research projects and their data dossiers reside in local workspaces (completely gitignored to preserve confidentiality and maintain a clean generic framework repo):

- **Project Workspaces:** Located in `projects/<project_slug>/` (e.g., `projects/neonatal_ab_stewardship/`, `projects/pediatric_prewarming/`, `projects/pmmr_crc_rt_ici/`, `projects/meta_hyperglycaemia/`).
- **Study Extraction Dossiers:** Study-level markdown audit dossiers with verbatim provenance are stored in local project folders (`projects/<slug>/data/study_dossiers/`).
- **Standardized Templates:** Blank extraction workbooks and PROSPERO forms are available in [`data/wiki/templates/`](file:///c:/Users/shuba/Desktop/Researcher%20-%20D%C3%B3ri/data/wiki/templates/).
